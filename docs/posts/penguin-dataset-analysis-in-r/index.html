Exploring the Gapminder Dataset with R

Author

Adnan Ali Syed

Published

September 28, 2026

Introduction

In this post, I will explore the Gapminder dataset using R. The goal is to look at differences in life expectancy and GDP per capita across continents, and then look at how life expectancy has changed over time for one country.

The data comes from the Gapminder dataset and is available in R through the gapminder package.

Load the packages

library(gapminder)
library(dplyr)
library(ggplot2)

The gapminder dataset contains information about countries across several years, including population, life expectancy, and GDP per capita.

Look at the most recent year

For the first part of the analysis, I will focus on the year 2007 and select only the variables that are useful for this comparison.

gapminder_2007 <- gapminder |>
  filter(year == 2007) |>
  select(country, continent, lifeExp, gdpPercap, pop)

Average life expectancy by continent

Next, I will group the countries by continent and calculate the average life expectancy for each continent.

continent_summary <- gapminder_2007 |>
  group_by(continent) |>
  summarise(
    average_life_expectancy = mean(lifeExp),
    average_gdp_per_capita = mean(gdpPercap)
  ) |>
  arrange(desc(average_life_expectancy))

continent_summary

This table allows us to compare the average life expectancy and GDP per capita across continents. The continents with higher average GDP per capita also tend to have higher average life expectancy in this dataset.

GDP per capita and life expectancy

We can also look at the relationship between GDP per capita and life expectancy for individual countries in 2007.

ggplot(
  data = gapminder_2007,
  mapping = aes(
    x = gdpPercap,
    y = lifeExp,
    colour = continent
  )
) +
  geom_point(size = 2) +
  labs(
    x = "GDP per Capita",
    y = "Life Expectancy",
    colour = "Continent"
  ) +
  theme_bw()

The scatter plot shows that countries with higher GDP per capita generally have higher life expectancy. There are also noticeable differences between continents.

Life expectancy in Canada over time

  filter(country == "Canada")

  data = canada_data,

    x = year,

The graph shows that life expectancy in Canada increased steadily over the period represented in the Gapminder dataset.

Conclusion

This analysis used the Gapminder dataset to explore life expectancy and GDP per capita. The 2007 data shows differences between continents and suggests a positive relationship between GDP per capita and life expectancy. Looking at Canada separately also shows that life expectancy increased over time.
